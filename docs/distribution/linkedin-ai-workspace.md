**Headline:** Stop treating Claude/ChatGPT/Copilot like a search bar.

**Body:**

The fastest upgrade most People teams can make this quarter doesn't involve buying anything. It's setting up the AI workspace properly.

Out of the box, Claude or ChatGPT is a stranger. Useful, but generic. The team uses it for drafts, summaries, the odd bit of research. The output is fine and forgettable.

Spend an afternoon configuring it as a colleague and the same tool starts producing work that sounds like your team:

- Custom instructions per function (tone, audience, what to never do)
- Projects with the documents the work actually depends on (policies, comp bands, role frameworks)
- Reference docs the model rereads on every reply
- Shared prompts the team can iterate on, not retype

The shift is from "I asked the AI" to "our AI did the first pass on this." That's the difference between a search bar and a workspace.

Full walkthrough here, with concrete examples for People functions on Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, and Gemini:

→ https://deepgrain.ai/intelligence/setting-up-your-ai-workspace

This is rung two on what we call the AI operating ladder. Most teams should be here before they try to build agents. Skipping it is the most common reason the agent project stalls six months later.
