**Title:** Show HN: AI Operating Index — a free 8-pillar diagnostic for AI readiness

**Body:**

I run an organisational consultancy that does a lot of AI readiness work. After enough engagements, the same diagnostic kept coming up: people grade themselves on the wrong axis (which model? which platform?) when the predictive axis is operational (data, tools, agents, governance, cadence, leadership, talent, intervention readiness).

So we built the diagnostic as a free standalone tool. Eight pillars, score 1-5 on each, get a tiered report back with what to do next.

It's not gated, it's not an upsell funnel (you don't even have to give an email if you don't want one). The longer-form thinking behind it is here: https://deepgrain.ai/intelligence/what-is-an-ai-operating-system

Curious what HN thinks of:
- The pillar framing (have we missed one?)
- Whether the tiered output is useful or feels generic
- Whether anyone here has built something similar internally and would push back on the model
