# Indie Hackers cross-post: AI Operating Index

---

**Title:** I built a free AI readiness diagnostic. Lessons from 3 months of consulting feedback.

**Body:**

After running enough AI readiness conversations, I noticed leadership teams kept asking the wrong question: "which model should we use?"

The predictive question is operational, not technical. So I built a free diagnostic that scores companies on the eight pillars that actually predict whether AI will compound: data, tools, agents, governance, cadence, leadership, talent, intervention readiness.

It's live at https://aioi.deepgrain.ai. No login wall, no upsell, no email required.

A few things I learned building it:

1. People want a number more than they want a report. Three minutes to a tier label is the right shape.
2. Showing the worst pillar matters more than showing the average. The weakest pillar decides the whole.
3. Nobody finishes a 40-question quiz. We got it down to 24.

Happy to dig into any of those if useful. Also curious what other indie folks have built around AI readiness, especially anything self-serve.

Long-form thinking behind it: https://deepgrain.ai/intelligence/what-is-an-ai-operating-system
