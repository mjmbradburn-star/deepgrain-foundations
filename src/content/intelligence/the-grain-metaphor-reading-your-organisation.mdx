## What the grain is, in an organisation

The grain is the pattern of how things actually move: which Slack channels carry the real decisions, which meetings are theatre and which are signal, which people are quietly load-bearing.

## How to read it

Walk the floor. Sit in the standups. Read six months of retro notes. Ask one question and listen to the answer for an hour.

<KeyTakeaways>
- Walk the floor. Read six months of retros. Sit in the standups. Most of the grain is invisible from the boardroom.
- Identify the load-bearing people. They are rarely on the org chart in the place you expect.
- Map the gap between the operating story and the operating reality. The gap is the engagement.
</KeyTakeaways>
