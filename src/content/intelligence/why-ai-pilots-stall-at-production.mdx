A pilot proves a model can do a task. Production proves an organisation can absorb the consequences. Most can't, yet.

The missing layer is almost always the same: there is no [AI operating system](/intelligence/what-is-an-ai-operating-system) underneath the pilot. The data pipeline is manual. Governance is a Slack thread. Nobody owns maintenance. The pilot demos in October and is dead by January.
