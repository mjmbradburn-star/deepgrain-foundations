Most organisations grade themselves on the wrong axis. The question is not "which model are we using" but "what does our data, tooling, agency, governance, and cadence look like."

These five pillars are also the spine of an [AI operating system](/intelligence/what-is-an-ai-operating-system). Readiness without an AI OS is a checklist. An AI OS without readiness is a runtime with nothing to run.
