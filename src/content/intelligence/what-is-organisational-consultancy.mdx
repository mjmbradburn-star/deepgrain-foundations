Most consulting sells answers. Organisational consultancy sells better questions — and the discipline to live with the answers once they arrive.

## The grain, not the diagram

Every organisation has a grain: the way decisions actually move, the way work actually flows, the way trust is actually earned. Most leadership teams have never read it. They have an org chart, a strategy deck, a values poster. The grain is something else.

> Organisational consultancy is the practice of reading the grain — and then choosing where to cut with it, and where to cut against it.

## What it is not

- It is not change management.
- It is not a transformation programme.
- It is not a McKinsey deck with three horizons.

It is closer to craft than to strategy. You spend weeks listening before you touch anything.

## The three movements

1. **Read.** Diagnose the operating reality, not the operating story.
2. **Craft.** Design interventions that fit the grain.
3. **Scale.** Compound the work without breaking it.
